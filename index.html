About me

I was born and brought up in Thanjavur, in the heart of Tamil Nadu. A thousand years ago it was the capital of the great Chola kings. Their Brihadeeswarar Temple, the "Big Temple" built by Raja Raja Chola I, still rises over the town today. Thanjavur is also home to the Saraswathi Mahal Library, which keeps thousands of old palm leaf manuscripts. Growing up among these temples and old writings, I always felt close to the wisdom kept on palm leaves.

In 2015 I travelled to Vaitheeswaran Koil, a temple town in the same Kaveri delta known for its Nadi palm leaf libraries, and had my own reading there, in person. Sitting with the reader while he searched for my leaf is something I have never forgotten.

Today I also work with mypalmleaf as a translator, so I know the readings from the inside. mypalmleaf brings these readings online with live English translation, and I'm one of its partners. I'm sharing a tradition from my homeland so that people far from India can experience it too.

Questions

Does everyone have a leaf?
Not everyone. According to the tradition, people who feel a strong call to search often have one waiting.
What if my leaf isn't found?
Up to three attempts are included. If your leaf still isn't found, you get the reading price back, or you can choose to try again.
Do I have to travel to India?
No. The reading happens online over Zoom, wherever you live.
Does booking through this page cost more?
No. It's the same price as on mypalmleaf.com.
